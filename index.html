Day reading

Western aspect between the Moon and the lord of its nakshatra at the session open. Trine, sextile or conjunction is bullish; square or opposition is bearish; no aspect within the orb means no call. Other planets go into the month trend.

Votes come from Moon aspects that become exact during the session: sesquiquadrate and semi-sextile follow the sign, 9°, 108° and 144° are bullish, and the book-table cells apply by sign.         What 2,454 trading days show

        Each factor was computed at 9:15 IST for every Nifty session from 27 Apr 2015 to 7 Apr 2025 and compared with that day's close-to-close move. Big days are moves of 2% or more (48 rises, 63 falls). Factors were picked on 2015–2020 and checked on 2021–2025.

        Moon sign & aspects (experimental, added Nov 2026)

        Clean test: the rules were chosen using only Nifty 2012–2018, then scored on 2019–2025, which the choice never saw (market up 54.3% of days). Strict pick = rule beat the base rate in both 2012–15 and 2016–18; loose pick = beat it over 2012–18 as a whole.

        Rule, scored on 2019–2025Strict: daysRightLoose: daysRight

        The new rules on their own did no better than a coin toss, and the rules picked from 2012–18 differ from the ones picked using all years, a sign of chance patterns. The star-lord day trend held up (54.5%). Bullish confirmation days, where the star lord and the new aspects both read bullish, rose about 63% of the time, roughly 2 standard errors above normal on 100–170 days; bearish confirmation failed. Before this clean test, the same rules scored 55.6% on 2016–25, overlapping the years used to choose them.

        Rules shown on the Day tab: Moon sesquiquadrate or semi-sextile any planet (rise in a bullish sign, fall in a bearish sign); Moon at 9°, 108° or 144° to the Sun, its star lord or any planet (rise); Moon in a bullish sign with Saturn, Mars or Sun in conjunction, 30°, 36°, 72°, 135° or 150° (rise), or with Uranus in 45°, 60°, 90°, 120° or 180° (rise); Moon in a bearish sign with Jupiter or Neptune in conjunction, 30°, 36°, 72°, 135° or 150° (fall). Bullish signs: Aries, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn (sidereal).

        Search for a 60% rule

        Every single factor, every pair and the common triples (363 factors in all) were tried on 2015–2020. 515 combinations scored 60% or more there. On 2021–2025 the same rules averaged 52.2%, and a vote of all of them scored 52.7%. Run on shuffled returns, where no astro effect can exist, the search still found 328 pairs at 60% against 335 on real data.

        Machine learning on all factors: logistic regression 67.4% → 49.5%, random forest 65.2% → 51.6%, gradient boosting 78.9% → 49.2% (2015–20 → 2021–25).
